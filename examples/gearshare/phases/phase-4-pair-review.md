# Phase 4: Pair Review

**Suggested time**: ~15 minutes

---

## Objective

Review the feature you just built together, as if it were a pull request from a colleague.

---

## What to Do

1. Read your own diff first and say what you would flag if someone else wrote it.
2. Look for what the tests do not cover: a stranger, a deleted item, two requests at the same time.
3. Decide what you would change before merging, and what can wait.

---

## Success Criteria

- Reviews the change critically, including the AI-written parts
- Separates what must change from what is a preference
- Explains the trade-offs of the approach taken
